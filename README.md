# TailorPro Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

## Introduction
TailorPro respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect
- Email address (for registration and login)
- Password (stored securely using authentication services)
- Customer information entered by you
- Body measurements
- Invoice details
- Mobile number (optional)
- Profile information
- Optional UPI details such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm mobile number.
- Optional bank account details entered by the user.

## How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Save customer measurements
- Generate invoices
- Sync your data securely
- Improve app performance

## Data Sharing
TailorPro does not sell or share your personal information with third parties except where required by law or to provide essential services such as secure authentication and cloud storage.

## Data Security
We use reasonable security measures to protect your information.

## Data Deletion
Users may request deletion of their account and stored data by contacting us.
